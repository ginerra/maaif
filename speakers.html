Speakers

Hon. Grace Namutebi

Minister of State for Agriculture, MAAIF

Grace Namutebi oversees national policy for crops and export commodities. She has led reforms that improved farmgate prices for coffee growers.

Dr. Peter Okello

Director of Research, National Coffee Research Institute

Peter Okello has spent twenty years breeding disease-resistant coffee varieties. His work has helped farmers fight coffee wilt across Uganda.

Prof. Amina Kyomuhendo

Professor of Agronomy, Makerere University

Amina Kyomuhendo studies how climate change affects highland and lowland coffee. She advises smallholders on shade, water and soil management.

Sarah Atim

Head of Agribusiness, Pearl Development Bank

Sarah Atim designs loan products for farmer cooperatives. She has helped more than 300 groups gain access to affordable credit.

Call for Papers

We invite researchers, practitioners and students to submit abstracts on coffee production, processing, markets, climate resilience and policy. Abstracts should be 300 words or fewer and include the title, author names, affiliation and three keywords.

Submit your abstract as a PDF or Word file to papers@coffeeexpo2026.example. Accepted papers will be presented in the parallel sessions and published in the Expo proceedings.
