Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16–18 September 2026 | Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

Overview

The Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF) invites growers, roasters, traders, investors and policy makers to three days of trade, learning and tasting at the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026. Coffee is Uganda's leading export crop, and the Expo showcases the best of Robusta and Arabica from the Pearl of Africa.

Visitors can meet exhibitors from across the value chain, attend sessions on sustainable farming, quality, financing and export markets, and join cupping sessions led by certified Q-graders. Exhibitor booths are now open for registration.
